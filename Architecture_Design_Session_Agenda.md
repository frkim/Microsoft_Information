## Architecture Design Session Agenda

### 1. **Welcome and Introductions** (10 minutes)
   - Greeting and overview of the session
   - Introductions of all participants

### 2. **Session Objectives and Agenda Overview** (5 minutes)
   - Review of the session's goals and outcomes
   - Brief rundown of the agenda

### 3. **Customer's Business Goals and Requirements** (20 minutes)
   - Discussion of the customer's business objectives
   - Review of functional and non-functional requirements
   - Identification of key stakeholders and their needs

### 4. **Current Architecture Overview** (15 minutes)
   - Presentation of the existing architecture
   - Discussion of current performance, issues, and limitations

### 5. **Proposed Architecture Design** (25 minutes)
   - Detailed presentation of the proposed architecture
   - Discussion of components, technologies, and patterns
   - Highlighting key improvements and benefits

### 6. **Deep Dive into Key Components** (30 minutes)
   - In-depth review of critical components and services
   - Discussion of design decisions and rationale
   - Assessment of scalability, security, and maintainability

### 7. **Integration and Interoperability** (15 minutes)
   - Review of integration points with existing systems
   - Discussion of data flows and interfaces

### 8. **Risk Analysis and Mitigation Strategies** (10 minutes)
   - Identification of potential risks and challenges
   - Discussion of mitigation plans and fallback strategies

### 9. **Feedback and Q&A** (20 minutes)
   - Open floor for feedback from the customer
   - Q&A session to address any concerns or queries

### 10. **Next Steps and Action Items** (10 minutes)
   - Summary of key takeaways from the session
   - Assignment of action items and responsibilities
   - Scheduling follow-up meetings

### 11. **Closing Remarks** (5 minutes)
   - Final thoughts and thank you
   - Confirmation of contact details for further communication

**Total Duration: 2 hours 45 minutes**
